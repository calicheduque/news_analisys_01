## Where Narrative and Experience Diverge

The public narrative of "falling rents" is frequently driven by **landlord incentives** — free months, reduced fees — rather than actual list-price reductions that show up in headline statistics. Even where modest nominal drops exist, they are often swallowed by broader inflation, meaning **real rents are not falling for most New Yorkers.** Without private experience data to cross-reference, we cannot confirm whether tenants on the ground are actually *feeling* relief.
